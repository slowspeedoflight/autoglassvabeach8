A rock strike or sudden temperature change can leave a chip or crack in your windshield. When the damage is still small, professional repair is often the fastest, most affordable, and most effective solution. Why Repair a Chip or Crack Quickly?

Windshield damage rarely stays the same size. Vibration from driving, road bumps, and changes in temperature can cause a chip to spider or a short crack to grow across the glass. Repairing early can:

  • Prevent the need for a full windshield replacement
  • Restore structural strength to the damaged area
  • Improve visibility and reduce glare from the damaged spot
  • Save time and money compared with replacement
  • Often be covered by insurance with little or no deductible

Many insurance policies encourage repair because it is safer and less expensive than waiting until the glass must be replaced.

What Types of Damage Can Be Repaired?

Not every windshield problem can be fixed with a repair. In general, repair works best when:

  • The chip is smaller than about a quarter (or within manufacturer guidelines)
  • The crack is relatively short (often under 6–12 inches, depending on location and type)
  • The damage is not directly in the driver’s primary field of view in a way that blocks safe vision
  • The outer layer of glass is damaged but the inner layer is intact
  • Dirt and moisture have not heavily contaminated the break for a long period

Our technicians evaluate each chip or crack on site and give you an honest recommendation. If repair is not the right long-term solution, we will explain why and discuss replacement options.

How Mobile Windshield Repair Works

Our process is designed to be convenient and thorough:

  • Inspection – We examine the size, depth, and location of the damage.
  • Cleaning – The break is cleaned so resin can bond properly.
  • Resin injection – Professional-grade resin is injected into the chip or crack under controlled pressure.
  • Curing – The resin is cured (often with UV light) so it hardens and stabilizes the glass.
  • Finishing – The surface is polished so the repair is as smooth and clear as possible.

Most chip repairs can be completed in a short appointment. You do not need to visit a shop—we come to your home, workplace, or another convenient location in the Virginia Beach and Hampton Roads area.
