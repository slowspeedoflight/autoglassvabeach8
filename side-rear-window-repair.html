Side & Rear Window Repair and Replacement

Broken door glass, shattered rear windows, or damaged quarter glass — we provide mobile side and rear auto glass service in Virginia Beach and surrounding cities.

Side and rear windows take a different kind of abuse than windshields—door slams, break-ins, accidents, and flying debris can all leave you with broken or missing glass. Virginia Beach Auto Glass Repair handles side window and rear window repair and replacement for vehicles throughout Virginia Beach, Norfolk, Chesapeake, and the greater Hampton Roads region.

Types of Side & Rear Glass We Service

  • Door glass (front and rear doors) – Power or manual windows that roll up and down
  • Quarter glass – Fixed or vented glass panels on the sides of many vehicles
  • Rear window / back glass – Including glass with defroster lines
  • Vent windows and specialty side glass – On certain trucks, vans, and older models

Each type of glass has its own removal and installation considerations. Door glass must align with regulators and tracks; rear glass often includes defroster grids that must remain intact or be properly connected.

Common Reasons Side or Rear Glass Needs Replacement

  • Break-in or vandalism
  • Accident or impact damage
  • Glass that has come off the track or regulator
  • Failed defroster (in some cases requiring glass replacement)
  • Severe scratches or cracks that affect visibility or security

Our Approach to Side & Rear Glass Work

We focus on:

  • Using the correct glass for your vehicle’s make, model, and year
  • Careful handling of door panels, regulators, and weatherstripping when needed
  • Proper sealing so water and wind noise stay out
  • Respecting defroster connections on rear glass
  • Mobile service when the job can be completed safely on site

Some complex door-glass jobs may require more time or a controlled environment. We will tell you upfront what to expect.

Security and Weather Protection

Missing or broken side/rear glass is not only inconvenient—it leaves your vehicle vulnerable to weather and theft. We aim to schedule side and rear glass work promptly so you can secure your vehicle again quickly.
