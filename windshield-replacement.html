Windshield Replacement in Virginia Beach

When repair is no longer enough, we provide careful, professional mobile windshield replacement across Virginia Beach and the Hampton Roads area.

Some windshield damage cannot be safely or permanently repaired. Long cracks, damage in critical vision areas, edge cracks, or extensive star breaks often require a full windshield replacement. Virginia Beach Auto Glass Repair offers mobile windshield replacement so you can have the work done at your home, office, or another convenient location.

When Is Windshield Replacement Necessary?

Replacement is typically recommended when:

  • A crack is long or is spreading toward the edge of the glass
  • Damage sits in the driver’s primary line of sight and cannot be repaired clearly
  • There are multiple chips or cracks across the windshield
  • The damage reaches or starts at the edge of the glass (which can compromise structure)
  • Previous repairs have failed or the glass is heavily pitted or scratched
  • The vehicle’s advanced driver assistance systems (cameras/sensors) require a new, properly calibrated windshield

We inspect the glass and explain your options clearly so you can make an informed decision.

What Goes Into a Quality Windshield Replacement

A proper replacement is more than “putting in new glass.” It involves:

  • Correct glass selection – Using the right OEM or high-quality equivalent windshield for your vehicle’s year, make, and model
  • Safe removal – Carefully removing the old glass and old adhesive without damaging the vehicle’s pinch weld or paint
  • Surface preparation – Cleaning and preparing the frame so the new urethane bonds correctly
  • Proper adhesive – Using automotive-grade urethane designed for structural windshield bonding
  • Correct set and cure time – Allowing the adhesive to cure according to specifications before the vehicle is driven
  • Sensor and camera considerations – Accounting for rain sensors, forward-facing cameras, and ADAS systems when applicable

The windshield is a structural part of your vehicle. It supports the roof in a rollover and helps the passenger airbag deploy correctly. Quality installation matters for safety.

What to Expect After Replacement

  • You will receive guidance on safe drive-away time (how long before the vehicle should be driven)
  • We recommend avoiding car washes and high-pressure water on the edges for a period after installation
  • Keep the tape or retainers in place if they were used, until advised to remove them
  • If your vehicle has ADAS cameras, ask about calibration requirements for your specific model
